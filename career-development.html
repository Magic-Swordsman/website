Career Development: Computer Engineering (Co-op) at University of Waterloo

Embarking on a 5-year journey at the University of Waterloo, I am pursuing a Bachelor of Applied Science in Computer Engineering with a Co-op option.

Program Overview

Throughout the program, I will delve into various aspects of computers, from hardware components like chips and wiring to software development, networks, and communications. Co-op terms will provide me with 2 years of paid work experience, preparing me for a versatile career path as a software developer, hardware engineer, IT specialist, systems designer, and more.

Admission Requirements

To secure admission into this program, I must meet specific academic criteria:

  • Ontario Secondary School Diploma (OSSD)
  • Grade 12 English (ENG4U)
  • Grade 12 Advanced Functions (MHF4U)
  • Grade 12 Calculus and Vectors (MCV4U)
  • Grade 12 Physics (SPH4U)
  • Grade 12 Chemistry (SCH4U)
  • One additional Grade 12 university-level or university/college-level course

A minimum average in the high 80s to low 90s is required, and a minimum grade of 70% is necessary in all prerequisite courses. Additionally, an Admission Information Form (AIF) is required for admission.

Program Details

  • Program Length: 5 years
  • Tuition (In-Province): $16,000 for 1 year(s)
  • Tuition (International): $42,000 - $63,000 for 1 year(s)
  • Co-op Option: Yes
  • Part-Time Study Option: No
